Reproducible evaluation

Measure reconstruction. Do not make unsupported claims.

PredixaLearn ships a synthetic regression fixture and a benchmark harness. Real-world results are published only after permission-cleared papers and manually verified ground truth are available.

What is measured

Character and word error, question-number accuracy, reading order, equation/table/figure handling, traceability, processing time, correction time, and AI-grounding rate.

Current evidence

The bundled Judge Demo is an original synthetic fixture for regression testing. It is not presented as a real-world accuracy benchmark.

Follow the reproducible manifest and report instructions in the project documentation, then record hardware, OCR/model versions, source permissions, and known limitations.
