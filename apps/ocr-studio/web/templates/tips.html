03 / TROUBLESHOOTING

Symptom, likely cause, next action

Text is missing or inaccurate

Confirm that the document is in English, use the original PDF, improve lighting and focus, and try the Exam profile only for question papers.

A table is marked Needs review

The grid did not pass source-coverage or plausibility checks. Use the visible OCR evidence and crop, or rescan more squarely; CSV/XLSX are withheld rather than guessed.

DOCX is partial or unavailable

Download Markdown and JSON first, then check Warnings and Service health for LibreOffice or conversion details.

Processing appears slow

The {{ device }} queue runs one GPU inference job at a time. Large PDFs, adaptive retries, DOCX generation, and local render validation can take several minutes.

04 / PRIVACY

OCR is local. GPT analysis is opt-in.

  • Original uploads are deleted after completion, failure, or cancellation.
  • History retains result bundles plus bounded, watermarked page previews and question crops—not the original source document.
  • Without consent, question segmentation, History, exports, and local review never contact OpenAI.
  • With consent, PredixaLearn sends only bounded selected question text, page/line references, marks, and OCR confidence. It never sends the source file, images, local paths, or all History.
  • OPENAI_API_KEY is read at runtime only. It is never shown in the browser or stored in History.
  • Deleting a History record removes its archive but does not remove the latest named export.
  • Run again restores settings and always asks you to choose the source file again.

05 / EVIDENCE AND REVIEW

Every suggestion must lead back to OCR evidence

Analyze segments page-ordered OCR lines deterministically. Each question keeps its display number, source page, line IDs, geometry, explicit or inferred marks, and OCR confidence. GPT suggestions are validated locally; unknown questions, pages, or line IDs are rejected.

Teacher edits override AI fields, are retained as separate local review data, and are appended to an audit log. Practice suggestions are always marked for teacher review. Historical patterns are revision priorities—not predictions of future exam questions.

06 / ADVANCED

How verification and export work

Figures and screenshots

Content figures are cropped with {{ crop_padding_pixels }} pixels of padding and embedded up to the configured {{ max_embedded_images }}-image ceiling. Invalid crops fall back safely and generate a warning.

Correction and watermark removal

Offline correction is conservative and protects names, codes, formulas, URLs, and structured identifiers. Optional removal terms are literal, case-insensitive, and retained as raw evidence in JSON.

Tables and formulas

Only accepted normalized table grids feed Markdown, DOCX, CSV, and XLSX. Unsafe formula OCR remains visible as literal text and is reported for review.

Output validation

DOCX conversion coverage checks content transfer. Project-local LibreOffice then verifies that the document opens and renders; it does not claim the OCR itself is accurate.

07 / SERVICE HEALTH

Understand the status badge

Initializing means you can prepare a file while the OCR engine loads. Ready means the requested device and local export toolchain are available. Degraded identifies an issue worth correcting, while completed OCR results remain protected.
